# Training session and stopwatch (#27; CS-2, CS-4)

For the developer. One session of about **1 hour** at the shop, on **trial data** (TREINO badge in the header), in the last week before the go-live. Bring a printed [`guia-rapido.md`](guia-rapido.md) and a stopwatch (phone). What the owner hears is in pt-BR below.

## Before the session

- [ ] The latest version is installed and the system is in **trial mode** (`data-treino\`).
- [ ] The trial database has the **real** tabs, methods, machines and fees (or close to them), so the training looks like the real days.
- [ ] Take some real sales from a recent day of the owner's Excel to type in (paper or printout).

## Agenda (≈ 60 min)

| Min | Topic | Owner does |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Opening the system: desktop shortcut, Início, Alt+number, Enter/Tab/Esc. | Opens it from the shortcut. |
| 5–20 | **Sale** (guide "Venda"): value → tab (digit or letters) → method → machine/installments → Enter. Split payment = two sales. Edit/delete today's sale. | Types 10–15 sales from his Excel. |
| 20–25 | **Refund** of a sale from another day. | One refund. |
| 25–35 | **Expense** with and without "Pago com dinheiro do caixa"; **sangria**; a **fixed bill** paid from the till. | One of each. |
| 35–45 | **Close the day**: count, difference and note, then **reopen** with a reason and close again. Day report. | Closes and reopens. |
| 45–55 | **Stopwatch**: CS-2 (10 sales) and CS-4 (closing), below. | |
| 55–60 | What to do if something goes wrong (guide); how the parallel period works ([`conferencia.md`](conferencia.md)); questions. | |

## CS-2: a sale in ≤ 10 s (average of 10)

- Use 10 real sales from his Excel, mixed like a normal day (cash, debit, credit with installments, PIX). Read each one out loud (value, tab, method).
- **Start** when you finish reading the sale; **stop** when the "Venda de R$ … registrada." message appears.
- Do it **after** the training part, not as the first try. If the average is over 10 s, note which step is slow (finding the tab? the machine?) and repeat once at the next visit.

## CS-4: closing the day in ≤ 3 min

- The trial day has its sales, expenses and at least one sangria.
- **Start** when the owner opens Fechamento (Alt+5) with the cash already in front of him; **stop** when the day shows "Dia fechado". Counting the notes and coins is part of it.
